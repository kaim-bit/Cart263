All of the work that I have done has been very enjoyable. I will try to talk about features and mechanics that I have seen throughout the submissions that I have found enjoyable. What immediately comes to mind is Benjamin Merhi’s submission. A game that shines light on combat sport athlete abuse. The main feature being a 3d enemy boxer that you have to defeat with your own punches. This alone is quite impressive, it came with animations, sound effects and a health bar. It is quite a quick game but I believe that the content balances the scales. Another work that has to be included in this review is Word Nerd. This game already swept the competition in the midterm game jam and now its multiplayer with a much more intricate and slick design. The main attraction now being that it is a multiplayer game. It also contains much more art than its first version as well as more scoring mechanics. This game is all around very solid, with great design, good ideas and very nice mechanics. I wanted to include this work because I was very fortunate in having the chance of playing this game with my class. It was very enjoyable and impressive. The next project I looked at was Can You See Me?, i was very soothed by the cute 3d autumn hill that was displayed. The controls were a bit strange especially when you rotated the camera position to better view the ghost. Other than that i enjoyed the presentation very much as well as the gif animations that were used to signify the start and finish of the game, i believe that those alone conveyed the overall mood well. The font was also a very nice touch. My personal favorite, Escapism, is more like an interactive art piece. Immediately when starting you are thrown into this little mountainous green paradise with countless flamingos coming from the ground. The color scheme as well as the 3d models makes this really enjoyable, the animations for the birds are nice and the sheer numbers communicate a feeling of joy very efficiently. I don't have much to criticize about the game. I like that it is just this scene in which consumers can enjoy, maybe adding a better border or a fog to hide the geography cut would be beneficial but i dont think that it drags the experience down by a big amount. Overall this project is very nice and i am happy to have gotten the chance to see it. The most technically impressive in my opinion is the 3D snake game. This game uses the sphere point of view that we learned in class very well and adds a grid like pattern in the middle, as well as a working snake game that has everything that the original holds, food, growing mechanic, as well as self collisions. The icing on the top is definitely the animated sky box. Overall a great project that they should be proud of. All of these projects were great and i think that this semester was a great success.
